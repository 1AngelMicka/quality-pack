## Script

1. "Thank you for your time. We are testing the product, not you: nothing you do is wrong. Please say what you think as you go."
2. "I will not help during the tasks, even if you ask. That is how we find what is unclear."
3. If recording: "May I record the screen? You can say no." Wait for the answer.
4. Read task 1 aloud, exactly as written. Then stay silent and take notes.
5. If they ask what to do: "What would you do if I were not here?"
6. After each task: "How was that?" Nothing more.
7. At the end: "What was the hardest?" and "What did you expect that was not there?"
8. Thank them.

## Notes, per person and per task
Time started and ended. What they clicked first. Where they stopped. What they said, in their words. Whether it was done unaided, done with trouble, or failed.
